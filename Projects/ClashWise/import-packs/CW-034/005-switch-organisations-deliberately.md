## Work across more than one organisation

@action: Use the prepared account's organisation switcher to move from Northstar Training to a second synthetic organisation and back.

A user can belong to more than one organisation. The active organisation determines the subscription context, people, projects, and policy shown in the app.

Before inviting, configuring policy, or checking a missing project, confirm the active organisation. Similar project names across different organisations can otherwise make an access problem look like missing data.

Switch back to Northstar Training before continuing.
