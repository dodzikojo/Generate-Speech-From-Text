## Verify the rendered report

@action: Generate, open the downloaded PDF, and inspect the cover, twelve-row summary, and one clash page.

Confirm the report title, cohort count, clash numbers, image, coordination fields, comment, and provenance. Missing viewpoint images trace back to publish capture, not to the PDF renderer inventing geometry.

A clash report is different from a session PDF, which reports one meeting's attendees, reviewed clashes, activities, attachments, and minutes.

## Match the format to the next system

Excel preserves a readable table with formatting and is the best general handoff for people who will sort, filter, or annotate. C S V is a plain table for scripts and systems that do not need workbook styling.

B C F two point one is for issue exchange with compatible B I M tools. It carries topics and available viewpoints or snapshots; it is not a full model and does not guarantee every receiving tool supports every field identically.

P D F is a fixed human-readable record. It is easy to circulate but should not be treated as a live source of truth after coordination continues.

@action: Open each prepared output and compare the first, last, and total permanent clash numbers with the twelve-row source cohort.

Check configured priority and status values, responsible contact, due date, comments or descriptions where included, and the expected image or viewpoint support.

Large exports continue in the background. Use the tray to read queued, processing, ready, warning, failed, or cancelled state. Download only after the job reports Ready, and keep any warning with the handoff so the recipient knows what was omitted.
