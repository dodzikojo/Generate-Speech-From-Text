## The finished setup

@visual: Open on Navisworks with the ClashWiseAI ribbon visible, then show the signed-in ClashWise dashboard and organisation switcher.

By the end of this lesson, the ClashWiseAI ribbon will be available in Navisworks Manage, and the plugin and web app will be using the same ClashWise account.

Before you begin, confirm three things. You are on a supported 64-bit Windows computer. You have Navisworks Manage installed, not Navisworks Freedom. And you can close Navisworks while the plugin is installed.

At the time of this recording, ClashWise supports Navisworks Manage 2024, 2025, 2026, and 2027. Compatibility changes over time, so use the linked installation article to check the current list before downloading.

## Register and verify your email

@action: Open the ClashWise registration page in a clean browser profile and complete the form with the prepared training identity.

Go to clashwise dot ai slash register. Enter your name, work email, and time zone. For this training environment, I am using coordinator at northstar dot example.

Choose your time zone carefully. ClashWise uses it for session schedules, due dates, notifications, and every other date shown in the web app. You can change it later from your profile.

Select Create account. ClashWise sends a verification email rather than asking you to create a password in the registration form. Open that message, choose Set your password, and enter a password of at least eight characters.

When verification succeeds, ClashWise signs you in and opens the dashboard. If the link has expired, register again with the same email to receive a fresh one. Keep password recovery and exact email-delivery troubleshooting in the help article, where those steps are easier to search.

Registration normally creates a personal organisation with you as its administrator. If a colleague invited you before you registered, the same password-setting step can also activate the reserved membership. In either case, use the organisation switcher after sign-in to confirm where you are working. Do not assume the first dashboard you see is the project organisation you intend to use.

If the time-zone prompt appears, complete it before continuing. This is not a cosmetic preference. A session scheduled for nine in the morning must display as nine in the morning for the people running it, and due-date warnings use the same setting.
