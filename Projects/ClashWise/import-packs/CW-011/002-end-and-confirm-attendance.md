## Open the wrap-up step

@action: Choose End session and review Confirm who attended.

Ending stops automatic recording and closes guest access, so ClashWise asks for a final roster check first.

The attendee list distinguishes people who joined the room from people who were only invited or listed. Correct a name, add someone who joined outside the browser experience, or remove a rehearsal identity that should not appear in the record.

If parallel lanes were used and some work was unfinished, the wrap-up can offer follow-up actions for uncovered lanes. They are suggestions, not automatic outcomes. This first session did not split the room.

## Confirm and end

@action: Choose Confirm roster and end, then show the ended state and minutes generation status.

Confirm the roster and end the session. The LIVE indicators disappear and automatic change capture stops.

ClashWise queues Wise to generate minutes from the recorded evidence. The panel updates when they are ready; there is no need to refresh repeatedly.

Decisions and action items remain editable after the session ends so the chair can correct the record. Clash fields can also continue to change, but post-session clash edits are not retroactively added to this meeting.
