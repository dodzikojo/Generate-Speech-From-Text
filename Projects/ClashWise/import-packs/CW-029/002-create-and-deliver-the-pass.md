## Invite the synthetic fabricator

@action: Choose Invite on Openings and use fabricator at northstar dot example.

ClashWise mints the pass, emails the join link when delivery is available, and shows a one-time copy for direct handoff. The lane transfers to the named guest on the board.

The link itself is the credential. There is no second password. Never zoom the raw token in the address bar, clipboard history, captions, or archive name.

## Understand validity and replacement

@screen: Show the pass state and Re-invite control without exposing the token.

The pass works only while the session remains Active and also has a limited abandoned-meeting lifetime. Verify the current duration in the deployed access brief or help article.

Re-invite creates a new link and revokes the old one. Inviting a second guest to the same lane replaces the first.
