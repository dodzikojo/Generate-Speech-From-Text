## Choose the share by recipient question

An insight share answers, What is happening across this selected coordination scope? It presents dashboard tiles and trends through the saved layout.

An external responsibility link answers, Which clashes are assigned to me? It is generated through notification routing and exposes only that contact's relevant read-only rows for one set.

Neither link grants project membership. Neither should be described as a substitute for a signed-in collaborator who needs full context and editing access.
