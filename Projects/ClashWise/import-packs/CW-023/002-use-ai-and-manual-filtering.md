@screen: Open the manual builder, show A, B, or Either element sides, All properties, and one prepared spatial condition.

Manual filters can nest AND and OR groups, target one side of the clash, use any published property, and express geometry such as above, below, inside, adjacent, or within a clearance.

For this cohort, keep the production filter readable. Add complexity only when it changes membership in a way the team can explain.
