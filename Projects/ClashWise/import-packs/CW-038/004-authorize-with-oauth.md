## Sign in without exposing a secret

@action: Select Refresh, choose the ClashWise data source, and move to the prepared browser consent screen.

Power BI opens ClashWise OAuth in the browser. Sign in with the normal synthetic ClashWise account. Review the requested read-only reporting permission and approve it.

No client secret is displayed, copied, or pasted. Keep password-manager prompts, browser autofill, tokens, and unrelated tabs outside the capture.

@action: Return to Power BI and allow all ten tables and five report pages to load.

The connector sees only projects available to the signed-in account. If a project is missing, verify organisation and project membership in ClashWise rather than trying to change the report query.
