## Preview a low-noise result

@visual: Show the prepared profile with Assignments immediate, general activity on a digest, and Archive Test Set muted.

Notifications should surface responsibility without turning every bulk operation into noise.

ClashWise groups activity into five categories: assignments, due dates, activity on your sets, coordination sessions, and publish and analysis results. Each person can choose an email cadence and whether the category appears in the in-app feed, within the limits set by their organisation.

A practical starting point is immediate email for direct assignments, daily digest for broader activity, and in-app visibility for categories you want to review when you are already working.

## Understand the delivery layers

Notification decisions resolve from the broadest safety control to the personal preference. The organisation master switch and hard-disabled categories come first. Then a set-wide mute, your personal set mutes, and your category settings apply.

Your Operational emails preference sits above notification email. If it is off, the in-app feed can continue while notification email stops.
