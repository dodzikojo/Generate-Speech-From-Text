## Configure the organisation guardrails

@action: Switch to the Admin profile, open Organization, and move to the Notifications settings.

The organisation master switch is the emergency brake. Leave it on for normal operation and use category policy for finer control.

For each category, Member choice supplies a default while allowing personal overrides. Off by default begins quiet but lets a member opt in. Disabled for everyone is a hard stop that members cannot override.

Set a due-soon lead time that matches the team's coordination rhythm. Weekly coordination often needs enough warning for an owner to act before the next meeting.

Save the policy, then return to the member profile to confirm how inherited and disabled states appear.
