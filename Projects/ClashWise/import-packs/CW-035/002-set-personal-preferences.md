## Configure each category

@action: Open Profile, scroll to Clash notifications, and change the prepared categories.

For each category, choose Immediate, Hourly, Daily digest, Weekly digest, or No email. Then decide whether it belongs in the in-app feed.

Set Assignments to Immediate so new responsibility is hard to miss. Put Activity on my sets and Publish and analysis on a digest for this training profile. Keep Coordination sessions visible in-app.

Until you choose a value, the selector inherits the organisation default. Once you choose, it becomes your preference. A later default change does not silently replace it.

Digest delivery follows the time zone on your profile. Verify that zone before relying on morning or weekly timing.

## Know what your choices cannot override

If an Admin has disabled a category for everyone, it is greyed out. Your stored preference remains but cannot take effect until the policy is relaxed. Personal settings also cannot override the organisation master switch or a set-wide mute.
