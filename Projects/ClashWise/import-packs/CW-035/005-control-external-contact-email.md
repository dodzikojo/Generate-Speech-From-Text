## Make external email an explicit decision

@action: Show External contact emails in the organisation notification settings, initially off.

Responsible contacts in a matrix may not be ClashWise users. Emailing those external people is off by default and only an Admin can opt the organisation in.

When enabled, an external contact can receive assignment and due-date email at the organisation's chosen cadence. Their secure link is read-only and limited to the clashes assigned to them in that set. It is not a project invitation and does not open the 3D viewer.

Before enabling it, confirm the matrix contains the real accountable people and accurate addresses. Use a daily digest unless a stronger operational reason requires another cadence.

## Understand member versus external routing

An active organisation member with access to the set's project follows the member route and their own preferences. A member without project access can still follow the external route; assigning responsibility never grants project access automatically.

Links expire and are rechecked against the live set. Muting the set or revoking its links can cut access immediately.
