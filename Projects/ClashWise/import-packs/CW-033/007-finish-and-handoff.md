## Know what deletion means

@action: Return as Owner, open Settings, and show the Delete project confirmation without completing it.

Deleting a project deletes the workspace, not its clash data. Owned clash sets revert to the personal model under their creators, and other members lose the access the project supplied.

Before deletion, review the Members tab and record who depends on that access. The operation is not a way to delete published clashes.
