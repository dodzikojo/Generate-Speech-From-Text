## Preview three lanes and one coverage board

@visual: Show the live Riverside room split into three prepared groups with six clashes each and the combined coverage total.

Splitting does not create separate meetings. It applies lane filters inside one live session, while every reviewed clash, field change, note, decision, and action continues into the same record.

The shipped workflow creates lanes from a property already present on the set. It does not ask the facilitator to type arbitrary lane names during the split.

## Open the Split menu

@action: As the driver, choose Split and inspect the available axes and projected lane counts.

Only the session starter or current driver can split. ClashWise offers axes that actually divide the loaded clashes: contact, group, level, grid, discipline, category, test, priority, status, tag, or the current multi-selection.

For the training data, choose Group because the prepared groups are Structure plus M E P, Architecture plus M E P, and Openings. Each contains six clashes.
