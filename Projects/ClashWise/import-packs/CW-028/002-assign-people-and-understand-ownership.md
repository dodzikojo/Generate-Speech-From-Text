## Claim and assign lanes

@action: Claim one lane and use Assign on the other two for the prepared participants.

Unclaimed lanes show a question-mark avatar and Take this lane. Assign can use people in the room, session attendees, and organisation members.

Contact lanes can assign themselves when the responsible contact's email matches someone in the room. A person owns at most one lane in the current deal; switching releases the previous lane.

## Treat a lane as responsibility, not permission

@screen: Show Your lane, its progress, and Show everything.

The viewer isolates your lane so you can focus, but it is a filter, not a fence for signed-in members. Show everything restores the wider set, and a member can inspect work outside the lane.

The coverage board records who owns the package. It is not an access-control boundary except for a fixed guest lane pass, which CW-029 covers.
