## Make independent progress

@action: In the second participant profile, open one lane clash, mark reviewed, change one field, save a decision or note, and add an action.

The participant works without following the main-room camera. The automatic field change and explicit outcome enter the same session record with that person's name.

The coordinator can inspect board progress without taking the lane or moving the participant's camera.

## Flag an issue for regroup

@action: On a clash that needs wider agreement, choose Flag for discussion.

Flagging parks the clash for the whole room instead of letting it disappear inside one lane. The lane row and board show the flag count.

Flag early. The regroup agenda is built from these issues and identifies who raised each one.
