## Open Coordination and schedule

@action: Choose Coordination from Riverside Cycle 01, then select Schedule.

The Coordination panel lists sessions for this set and offers Start now or Schedule. Choose Schedule when the meeting has a known time, agenda, and roster.

Enter the title Riverside Weekly Coordination, twenty-fourth September. Add a short agenda that names the Level zero three M E P review and unresolved high-priority clashes. Set the date, start time, and planned duration.

Scheduling places the meeting on the set's session list and the wider sessions hub. It does not start automatic recording.

## Add expected attendees

@action: Add the prepared coordinator, structure lead, and MEP lead using contact suggestions, then review their cards.

Type a name or email in Attendees. ClashWise can suggest contacts from the coordination data, and each attendee card allows you to verify name, company, and email.

Add the three synthetic participants and save. Their presence on the schedule means they are expected; it does not mean they joined. Later, the record distinguishes joined live, invited but did not join, and listed without joining.

Open the scheduled session once to verify the title, time in the current time zone, agenda, and roster. Decisions and action items remain unavailable until the session becomes active.
