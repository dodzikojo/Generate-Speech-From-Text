## Open Local AI

@action: From ClashWiseAI, General, choose Local AI; alternatively show Manage models from Title Update settings.

The AI Models manager requires a signed-in account with the appropriate naming entitlement. If Title Update opens with no active model, ClashWise can take you here automatically.

Filter by provider only when you are comparing a known model family. Otherwise, leave All providers selected and use the Recommended badge, languages, and workstation guidance.

The status distinguishes Not downloaded, a download percentage, Downloaded, and Active. Downloaded means the file is available; Active means Title Update will actually use it.

## Download and use the recommended model

@action: Choose Download on the recommended training model, cut through prepared progress states, then choose Use.

Start the download. The footer reports progress and provides Cancel Download. For a recording, show the beginning and a later prepared state rather than compressing a variable network transfer into a promise about speed.

When the download completes, choose Use. The status changes to Active. Close and reopen the manager once to prove the selection persists.

A model can be downloaded without being active. Always check the badge before a production Title Update run.
