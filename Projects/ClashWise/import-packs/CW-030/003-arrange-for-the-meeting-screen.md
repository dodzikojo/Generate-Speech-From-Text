## Unlock, resize, and order

@action: Unlock layout, move the headline tiles first, enlarge priority and owner charts, and place trends and aging below.

Arrange the layout for the laptop or meeting screen that will present it. Lead with outcome and risk, then responsibility and trend.

Resize labels until they remain legible at the planned browser zoom. Lock the layout afterwards to prevent accidental dragging during a meeting.

## Use tile toggles accurately

@screen: Switch Priority Analysis between AI recommended and Manual priority, and Owner Workload among Person, Email, and Company.

The tile toggles change what the chart represents. State the active source when discussing the result.

Insights does not provide a general custom date-range filter. Recent Activity and Aging use the fixed windows named by their tiles.
