## Start local generation

@action: Choose Start and show the Review Clash Titles window filling from pending rows to generated results.

The review window opens immediately with every in-scope clash represented. Pending rows show that generation has not reached them yet. Successful rows fill in as the local model responds.

The header reports completed versus total and adds an estimate after enough samples exist. For a larger run, Email me when generation finishes lets you move on without watching the window.

Stop halts new generation while preserving completed proposals for review and application.

## Understand each row state

@screen: Show generated, edited, failed, skipped, rejected, and pending examples in the prepared batch.

Generated means a proposal is ready and accepted by default. Edited means a person changed that proposal. Failed includes a reason and cannot be applied until a retry succeeds. Skipped explains why the row was outside the chosen settings, such as not new or previously updated. Rejected is a successful proposal you deliberately excluded. Pending is still waiting.

The filter chips and footer tally make a large batch auditable. A failed row does not invalidate good rows that have already completed.
