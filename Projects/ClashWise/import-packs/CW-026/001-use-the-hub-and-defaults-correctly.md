## Preview a prepared scheduled session

@visual: Show Riverside Weekly Coordination in the sessions hub with Scheduled status, then its set-level Coordination panel and roster.

Session preparation happens in two places. The organisation defines defaults for future meetings. The clash set's Coordination panel creates and manages the actual session.

The sessions hub is the cross-set calendar and history. In the current release it is reached directly at the coordination-sessions address rather than from the main drawer, and it does not replace the set-level scheduling flow.

## Read the hub

@screen: Show Live now, Scheduled, Completed, calendar views, and the All sessions table.

The hub summarises every accessible session with time, clash set, title, status, reviewed count, and minutes readiness. A row returns to the correct clash set with its Coordination panel open.

Use it to spot collisions in the meeting calendar and find an old PDF without remembering the source set. Access still follows the clash sets you can see.
