## Compare geometry styles

@action: In 3D mode, toggle Wireframe, X-ray, Exploded, and Orthographic one at a time, returning to the normal useful view after each.

Wireframe exposes edges in dense geometry. X-ray reveals hidden clashes through surrounding objects. Exploded separates pieces to explain their relationship. Orthographic removes perspective distortion for drawing-like inspection.

Use each mode to answer a question, then return to a stable view. Cycling effects without a purpose makes the geometry harder to interpret.

Orthographic is a camera projection inside 3D. Plan is a separate spatial review mode with one selected level, top projection, room outlines, labels, grids, and clash pins. Do not present those controls as the same feature.

## Choose what colour means

@screen: Step through Clash, Priority, Severity, Changes, Cluster, and Hotspots or Density.

Colour by Clash separates neighbouring issues. Priority uses the matrix's configured colours. Severity represents penetration depth. Changes distinguishes new, persisting, and recently resolved geometry. Cluster reveals spatial groups. Hotspots or Density highlights elements involved in many clashes.

State the active mode during a meeting; red can mean urgency in one mode and deep penetration in another.
