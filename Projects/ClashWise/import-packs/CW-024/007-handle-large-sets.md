## Understand the two thresholds

@visual: Open the prepared synthetic large cohort and show the focused-scene prompt and the truncated-state message.

Above one hundred and fifty clashes, ClashWise recommends a focused scene because smaller reviews are faster, clearer, and easier to share. You can still continue.

The hard viewer cap is one thousand clashes. A larger cohort loads only the first one thousand and displays a partial-count warning. The limit affects one scene, not the data stored in the clash set.
