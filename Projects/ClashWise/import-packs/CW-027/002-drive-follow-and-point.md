## Demonstrate the wheel

@action: Move the coordinator camera, select number thirty-one, then show the member following the same camera and clash.

The driver shares camera and current clash. Each participant keeps their own list, filters, and inspector.

The member asks to drive; the coordinator hands over. The member orbits, then stops driving. If no one holds the wheel, Take the wheel is a normal available state.

## Break away and return

@action: In the follower profile, orbit independently, then choose Follow coordinator.

Moving your own camera breaks follow automatically without affecting the driver. Follow returns you to the shared view.

Move the pointer over the model to show the labelled presence dot. Pointing is visible to others whether or not you drive.
