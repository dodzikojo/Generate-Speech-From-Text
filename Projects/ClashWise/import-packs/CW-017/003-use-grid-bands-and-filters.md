## Add grid or numeric buckets deliberately

@action: Add the prepared grid-zone or numeric property rule, open Bands, and auto-seed from the training values.

Exact grouping uses each property value as its own bucket. Bands turn numeric values such as elevation or offset into named ranges.

Auto-seed creates an initial set from the actual values. Review every minimum, maximum, and label. The first and last bands remain open-ended so values are not lost outside the sampled range.

For the Riverside story, use clear grid-band labels that a meeting chair can say aloud.

## Inspect unassigned results

@screen: Refresh preview and expand the ungrouped or unassigned result under the test.

The preview shows future group names, clash counts, and results that will remain ungrouped. Inspect that remainder instead of focusing only on the successful buckets.

An unassigned clash may have a blank property, fall below minimum size, or fail an include rule. Decide whether it needs a mapping fix, a broader bucket, or intentional individual review.

Do not force every clash into a misleading group just to make the unassigned count zero.
