## Compare the stuck and restored view

@visual: Show the intentionally isolated and coloured Riverside selection, then the full model restored to a consistent camera.

Many apparent plugin problems are view state. Elements are hidden, a selection remains isolated, a colour override is active, or the camera is somewhere unexpected.

The Visibility panel gives you a fast, repeatable recovery before you escalate a bug.

## Use View, Hide, Show All, and Reset

@action: Select the prepared elements, choose Hide to isolate them, then demonstrate View, Show All, and Reset.

View moves the camera to a consistent front-right-top perspective. Hide keeps the current selection and hides the rest of the appended model context.

Show All restores visibility but does not remove colour overrides or reset the camera. Reset performs the full cleanup: show all, remove temporary and permanent colours, clear selection, restore the standard view, and zoom to fit.

None of these controls changes model geometry or clash data.
