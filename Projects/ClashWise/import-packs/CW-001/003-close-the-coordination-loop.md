## Coordinate and capture what happened

@action: Start a prepared coordination session and make one status or responsibility change on a numbered clash.

When the team is ready to review, start a coordination session against the clash set.

While the session is active, ClashWise automatically records changes to status, priority, responsible contact, and due date, including the clash number and who made the change. You can also mark clashes reviewed, save decisions, create action items, add comments, and capture marked-up views.

When the session ends, Wise generates minutes from the activity that was actually recorded. The minutes are not a guess at what the team meant. They are built from saved changes, reviewed clashes, notes, decisions, action items, attendees, and attachments. You can then export the session report as a PDF.

## Sync, update, and repeat

@screen: Show the Sync summary in the plugin, then the updated fields on the matching clash in Clash Detective.

Back in Navisworks, Sync brings supported agreed values and groups onto matching clashes in Clash Detective. Matching depends on the clash identifier, so the same published document and clash results matter. The exact fields available also depend on the Navisworks version; we will cover those limits later in this series.

After the models change, rerun clash detection in Navisworks and use Update to refresh the existing set. Existing clash numbers, comments, decisions, shares, and history stay with the coordination record while new results enter the next review cycle.

That is the ClashWise loop: detect in Navisworks, publish, apply your matrix, analyze, triage, coordinate, report, sync, and update.
