## From detected clashes to coordinated decisions

@visual: Open on a completed ClashWise session record, then cut to the matching clash in Navisworks Clash Detective.

ClashWise turns detected clashes into coordinated decisions your team can act on.

The boundary is important. Navisworks Clash Detective finds the clashes. ClashWise does not run clash detection. Instead, it adds the management layer around those results: clearer names, consistent priorities, responsibility, review, meeting records, reporting, and a route back into Navisworks.

So you keep the detection workflow you already trust. ClashWise helps with what happens next: deciding which clashes matter, who owns them, what the team agreed, and whether that decision made it back into the coordination model.

## One product, two connected workspaces

@screen: Navisworks with the ClashWiseAI ribbon beside the ClashWise web dashboard.

The workflow moves between two connected workspaces.

Inside Navisworks, the ClashWiseAI ribbon prepares and publishes clash results. Its Clash Management panel gives you three important actions: Publish, Update, and Sync.

For a model-first cycle, the plugin can first scan the federation and connect it to a reviewed Clash Matrix, then create the search sets and clash-test definitions that Navisworks will run. After results are published, the web becomes the shared coordination workspace for analysis, triage, three-dimensional review, team decisions, and the record of the outcome.
