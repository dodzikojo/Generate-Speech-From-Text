## Choose cross-tab or table view

@screen: Toggle between matrix cross-tab and rules table.

The cross-tab is for scanning class or discipline pairs and applying values across a whole cell. The table is for precise rule-by-rule editing, search, sorting, activation, tolerance, tags, notes, and reasoning.

Use the pair cell to see the big picture, then switch to the filtered rule list when the details matter.
