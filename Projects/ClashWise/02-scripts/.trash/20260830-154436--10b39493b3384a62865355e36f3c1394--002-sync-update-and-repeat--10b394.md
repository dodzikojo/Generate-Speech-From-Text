Back in Navisworks, Sync brings supported agreed values and groups onto matching clashes in Clash Detective. Matching depends on the clash identifier, so the same published document and clash results matter. The exact fields available also depend on the Navisworks version; we will cover those limits later in this series.

After the models change, rerun clash detection in Navisworks and use Update to refresh the existing set. Existing clash numbers, comments, decisions, shares, and history stay with the coordination record while new results enter the next review cycle.

That is the ClashWise loop: detect in Navisworks, publish, apply your matrix, analyze, triage, coordinate, report, sync, and update.
