@action: Start a prepared coordination session and make one status or responsibility change on a numbered clash.

When the team is ready to review, start a coordination session against the clash set.

While the session is active, ClashWise automatically records changes to status, priority, responsible contact, and due date, including the clash number and who made the change. You can also mark clashes reviewed, save decisions, create action items, add comments, and capture marked-up views.

When the session ends, Wise generates minutes from the activity that was actually recorded. The minutes are not a guess at what the team meant. They are built from saved changes, reviewed clashes, notes, decisions, action items, attendees, and attachments. You can then export the session report as a PDF.
